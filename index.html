Features

Everything the comments section should have been.

Stop scrolling away from the video. Eavesdrop brings the conversation to the player.

🪟

A panel that floats

Drag it anywhere, resize it, snap it to a corner or minimize it to a bubble. Works in normal, theater and fullscreen.

⏱️

Comments about this moment

See what people say about the exact part you're watching, as the video plays.

🔗

Timestamps that jump

“2:47” in a comment takes the video right there. The community index shows the most-mentioned moments.

🆕

New since your last visit

Come back to a video and jump straight to the comments posted after you last watched.

🔎

Search, sort, mute

Search the comments, sort by likes, and blur comments with words you choose — spoilers stay hidden.

▶️

Auto-scroll

Let the comments scroll by themselves while you watch. One click pauses it.

Save & share

Keep the comments worth keeping.

Bookmark any comment with one click, and turn the best ones into images made for sharing.

  • Save with 🔖 — grouped by video, with the video's title.
  • Notes and tags — add your own context and find it later.
  • Export — Markdown for Notion or Obsidian, CSV or JSON.
  • Share as image — a clean card, ready for social media or chats.
Private by design

Your comments, your browser.

Eavesdrop has no servers and no accounts. Here's exactly what happens.

📡

Official YouTube API

Comments come from YouTube's public Data API. Only the video ID is sent to fetch them.

💾

Stays on your device

Settings, saved comments, notes and tags live in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

👀

See every request

A “Network activity” screen in Settings lists every request Eavesdrop makes.

FAQ

Questions

Does it cover the video or the subtitles?

You decide where it goes: drag it, resize it, snap it to a corner, lower its opacity or minimize it to a small bubble. It never covers the subtitles.

Do I need an account?

No. Not for Eavesdrop, and not for buying Pro — checkout only asks for an email to send your license key.

Does Eavesdrop collect my data?

No. It has no servers. Comments are fetched from YouTube's official API with only the video ID, and everything you save stays in your browser. See the privacy policy.

What happens if I reinstall Chrome or the extension?

Paste the same license key again in Settings. It's in the confirmation email from your purchase.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome and Chromium-based browsers that support Chrome Web Store extensions, like Edge and Brave.

What if Pro doesn't work for me?

If something isn't working for you, reply to your purchase email and we'll sort it out.
